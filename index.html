Police

Police

Emergency line — 24/7

911
Call Police

Report crimes, threats, or anything that feels unsafe.

When to call

  • Someone is threatening or following you.
  • A theft, assault, or break-in is happening or just happened.
  • You witness suspicious activity or a weapon.
  • You feel unsafe and cannot get away.

What to tell them

  1. Your exact location and nearest landmark.
  2. What happened and whether it is still happening.
  3. A description of the person and which way they went.
  4. Your name and a number they can call back.

While you wait

  1. Move to a lit, populated place or lock yourself in a room.
  2. Do not confront the person.
  3. Stay on the line until told to hang up.
  4. Use the SOS button to alert your contacts.

Fire Department

Fire Department

Fire & rescue — 24/7

911
Call Fire Department

Call for fires, smoke, gas leaks, and rescue situations.

When to call

  • You see flames, smell smoke, or hear an alarm.
  • There is a gas leak or a strong burning smell.
  • Someone is trapped or needs rescue.
  • An electrical fire or sparking equipment.

What to tell them

  1. The building name, floor, and room number.
  2. What is burning and if anyone is trapped.
  3. Whether you smell gas or see heavy smoke.
  4. Your name and callback number.

While you wait

  1. Get out first, then call from a safe distance.
  2. Stay low under smoke and use stairs only.
  3. Close doors behind you to slow the fire.
  4. Wait at the assembly point for responders.

Hospital

Hospital

Call Hospital

Serious injuries and medical emergencies need immediate care.

When to call

  • Chest pain, trouble breathing, or fainting.
  • Heavy bleeding, deep wounds, or broken bones.
  • Severe allergic reaction or poisoning.
  • Head injuries or seizures.

What to tell them

  1. Your location and the patient's condition.
  2. Age, allergies, and medications if known.
  3. Whether the person is awake and breathing.
  4. Your callback number.

While you wait

  1. Keep the person still, calm, and warm.
  2. Press firmly on any bleeding with a clean cloth.
  3. Do not give food or drink.
  4. Have someone meet responders at the entrance.

School Emergency

School Emergency

Campus security office

+1 555 987 6543
Call School Emergency

Campus security is the fastest help on school grounds.

When to call

  • Unsafe or suspicious people on campus.
  • Harassment, bullying, or threats.
  • Lost, stolen, or damaged property.
  • Facility hazards like broken locks or lights.

What to tell them

  1. Your building, room, or campus landmark.
  2. What is happening and who is involved.
  3. Your student name and ID.
  4. Where security can meet you.

While you wait

  1. Stay in a visible, well-lit area.
  2. Move toward other people or a staffed office.
  3. Keep your phone charged and on.
  4. Follow campus alerts and instructions.

Emergency Guide

Fire

  1. Alert others and pull the fire alarm.
  2. Leave belongings and exit via the nearest stairwell.
  3. Never use elevators.
  4. Stay low if there's smoke.
  5. Meet at the designated assembly point.

Earthquake

  1. Drop, cover, and hold on under sturdy furniture.
  2. Stay away from windows and heavy fixtures.
  3. If outdoors, move to an open area.
  4. After shaking stops, check for injuries and exit carefully.

Flood

  1. Move to higher ground immediately.
  2. Avoid walking or driving through flood water.
  3. Unplug electrical appliances if safe to do so.
  4. Follow official evacuation routes.

Medical Emergency

  1. Check responsiveness and call for help.
  2. Call emergency services or use the SOS button.
  3. Apply first aid only if trained.
  4. Stay with the person until help arrives.

Safety Tips

Campus Safety

  • Walk in well-lit, populated areas at night.
  • Save campus security's number in your phone.
  • Never prop open secured doors for strangers.
  • Use the buddy system for late classes or labs.

Online Safety

  • Avoid sharing your live location publicly.
  • Use strong, unique passwords for school accounts.
  • Turn on two-step verification where possible.
  • Be careful with links from unknown senders.

Travel Safety

  • Share your route with a friend before heading out.
  • Keep your phone charged before commuting.
  • Check the vehicle and driver details before riding.
  • Keep bags closed and valuables out of sight.

Dorm Safety

  • Always lock your door, even for quick errands.
  • Know your building's fire exits in advance.
  • Don't share your key or access card.
  • Report broken locks or lights right away.
