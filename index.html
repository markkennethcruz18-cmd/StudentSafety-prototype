Emergency Guide

Fire

  1. Alert others and pull the fire alarm.
  2. Leave belongings and exit via the nearest stairwell.
  3. Never use elevators.
  4. Stay low if there's smoke.
  5. Meet at the designated assembly point.

Earthquake

  1. Drop, cover, and hold on under sturdy furniture.
  2. Stay away from windows and heavy fixtures.
  3. If outdoors, move to an open area.
  4. After shaking stops, check for injuries and exit carefully.

Flood

  1. Move to higher ground immediately.
  2. Avoid walking or driving through flood water.
  3. Unplug electrical appliances if safe to do so.
  4. Follow official evacuation routes.

Medical Emergency

  1. Check responsiveness and call for help.
  2. Call emergency services or use the SOS button.
  3. Apply first aid only if trained.
  4. Stay with the person until help arrives.
